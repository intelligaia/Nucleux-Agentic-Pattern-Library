01 How it works

From one sentence to screens you can ship.

Describe your AI in plain words. Your agent connects to Nucleux, picks the patterns it needs and builds the screen with them — progress, sources and approvals included.

  1. 01

    Describe your AI

    Say what it does in your own words. No templates to fill in, no jargon.

  2. 02

    Get the right patterns

    Nucleux picks the screens and states your product needs, and tells you why each one is there.

  3. 03

    Ship it your way

    Hand it to your AI agent, build it in React, or design it in the kit. Same patterns either way.

    03 Why it matters

    A chat box isn't a product.

    People trust AI when it shows its work. Every AI product has to answer the same four needs — switch them on and watch the same conversation turn into a product.

    • Show it's working

      Processing steps

      A blank screen feels broken. Show progress and what the AI is doing right now.

      See the pattern

    • Show your sources

      Citations

      Every answer points back to where it came from, so people can check it.

      See the pattern

    • Ask before acting

      Consent

      Before it sends, deletes or spends, the AI asks — and says exactly what will happen.

      See the pattern

    • Recover gracefully

      Recovery

      When something fails, keep the person's work and offer one clear way forward.

      See the pattern

    06 Developers & AI agents

    Your AI agent already knows how to use it.

    Nucleux runs an MCP server, so Claude, Cursor, Codex and VS Code can ask it which patterns to use and build them for you. Prefer to write the code? It's one package.

    1. You

      Describe the screen

      In your coding agent: Claude, Cursor, Codex or VS Code.

    2. MCP

      Your agent asks Nucleux

      Which patterns fit, and when not to use them. nucleux.in/mcp

    3. Patterns

      It builds with them

      Real components from @nucleux/react, on your theme.

    4. Ship

      You review the plan

      Nothing changes in your files until you've seen it.

    Paste one prompt and your agent does the rest. Or install the package and compose the same patterns yourself — TypeScript and Tailwind throughout.
