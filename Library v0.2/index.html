Every stage opens onto a tighter set of design problems.

Inside each stage, patterns cluster around a handful of recurring conversations the team should have together — what the AI is, how the user enters, how the system responds, what changes over time.

Onboarding

Orientation before real use — what the AI is, who it is, what it can do, what we ask of users in return. Get this right and trust compounds across every later phase.

Browse 12 patterns
Trust & disclosure
What is this, what are the rules, what data does it use.
Identity
Who the AI is — visually, verbally, behaviourally.
Capability discovery
Showing — not telling — what the system can actually do.

Initially

First touch, first prompt. The moment the user actually engages — picks an entry point, gives the AI context, attempts their first ask. The job here is to make their first attempt succeed, not just exist.

Browse 24 patterns
Entry points
The doors users walk through to reach the AI.
Expressive input
Voice, visual, gesture, structured.
Prompt scaffolds
Helping users write a prompt that returns what they wanted.
Context expansion
Bringing the user's world into the AI's working memory.

During interaction

The bulk of the journey — output, processing, collaboration, refinement, error recovery, power controls. Most patterns live here, and most adoption is won or lost on the details.

Browse 37 patterns
Output & processing
How the AI responds, streams, paces, and shows its work.
Collaboration canvas
Shared work surfaces where user and AI co-edit.
Refinement
Iterating on output without starting over.
Explainability
Helping the user understand and trust the answer.
Recovery
When wrong — handling uncertainty, errors, and misalignment.
Power controls
Operators for users who push the system hard.

Over time

The long-term relationship. What the AI remembers, what it forgets, what changes, what stays the same. These patterns decide whether users come back — and whether trust survives the second month.

Browse 11 patterns
Memory & continuity
What persists across sessions.
Privacy & control
Surfaces that let users own their footprint.
Adaptation
How the system improves with use.
Change management
Telling the user when the system itself has changed.
