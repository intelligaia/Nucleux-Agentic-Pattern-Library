The framework

A pattern lives inside one of four moments.

Every stage opens a different design conversation. The library is organised against this map — so the right pattern is always one click away.

Stage 01 / 04

Onboarding.

Orientation before real use — what the AI is, who it is, and what it can do. Get this right and trust compounds across every later phase.

3 categories · 12 patterns
Stage 02 / 04

Initially.

First touch, first prompt — the moment the user actually engages. The job here is to make their first attempt succeed, not just exist.

4 categories · 24 patterns
Stage 03 / 04

During.

Active use and recovery — output, processing, refinement, and what happens when it slips. Most patterns live here, and most adoption is won or lost on the details.

6 categories · 37 patterns
Stage 04 / 04

Over time.

The long-term relationship — what the system remembers, what changes, what stays. These patterns decide whether trust survives the second month.
