Isometric studies.

Eight isometric directions for the mark — exploring depth, translucency, and gradient. Wordmark set in Fraunces italic for a more design-house feel against the geometric cube forms. Options 06–08 are the latest refinements: tighter overlap, softened corners, sized proportionate to the wordmark.

Iso cube · monochrome 01 / 08
The atomic form. Three faces in three values of ink — top is brightest, right is mid, left is darkest. Reads as a solid 3D tile. Foundational; the version you put on shipping crates and case studies.

Iso cube · brand faces 02 / 08
Each face takes one stop from the brand gradient — amber on top, violet on the right, indigo on the left. The cube holds the full palette in three flat planes. Strong personality at any scale.

Iso cube · gradient faces 03 / 08
Each face carries its own linear gradient — amber→rose on top, violet→rose on the right, indigo→violet on the left. Adds depth without losing the volumetric read. The most "live" of the iso variants.

Iso cube · translucent glass 04 / 08
Each face is a translucent gradient, edges traced in a hairline outline. Reads as a glass crystal — you can almost see through it. The most contemporary direction, sits naturally on the glass surfaces already in the site.

Iso stack · translucent layers 05 / 08
Three flat hexes layered with translucency and a slight iso offset between each — reads as plates of glass stacked at the same axis. Suggests strata: every pattern is a layer in a deeper system.

Iso mosaic · translucent overlap 06 / 08
Three iso cubes — softened corners, low-opacity fills, pushed in so they intersect. Where they meet, the brand palette mixes through the glass. Right-sized to the wordmark and rendered in Fraunces italic to give the lockup a craft / design-house tone.

Iso pair · two cubes 07 / 08
Just two cubes, sliding into each other. Warm amber against cool indigo — the two endpoints of the brand gradient. Quieter than the three-cube mosaic, reads cleaner at very small sizes (favicon, app icon).

Iso quartet · four cubes 08 / 08
Four iso cubes arranged in a honeycomb-shifted 2×2 — a real fragment of mosaic flooring. Holds the full brand gradient (amber → rose → violet → indigo) in a compact diamond. Most pattern-library-of-them-all, slightly denser than 06.

My pick

06 · Iso mosaic · translucent overlap as the primary mark — three softened cubes mixing through glass, sized to sit comfortably next to the wordmark, with Fraunces italic giving the lockup an editorial / craft tone. 07 · Iso pair at smaller scales (favicon, app icon, social avatar) where three cubes would collapse into mud — two cubes hold up at 16 px. Same atomic form, two scale treatments. Option 08 (quartet) is the version for marketing surfaces that need the full brand gradient in the mark.
