Sign In — interaction, validation and authentication states

Nine states of one screen, in the order a person meets them. Every frame is the live Sign In card captured from the running page in that state, so the layout, spacing, field and button dimensions are identical throughout — only what the state requires changes.

  • Inline message — the fault belongs to one field, and is said under it.
  • Form-level message — the fault belongs to the attempt; no field is marked.
  • Sign In — always pressable; disabled only while a submission is outstanding.
