Parental leave for adoptive parents

Handbook v4.2
How much paid leave do I get if we adopt? We're in the UK office.
MK
Pi
Pia from the Employee Handbook

In the UK office, adoptive parents get the same leave as birth parents:

  1. 26 weeks at full pay for the primary adopter 1
  2. Another 13 weeks at statutory pay if you want it 1
  3. 4 weeks at full pay for the second parent, taken any time in the first year 2

Tell your manager and HR within 7 days of being matched with a child. Leave can start up to 14 days before placement. 2
